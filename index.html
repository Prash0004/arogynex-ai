How it works & limitations


  • Synthetic data is generated by ml.py; the label is made up, so metrics show the pipeline works, not clinical accuracy.
    • 

  • Train/test split is grouped by participant to prevent leakage. Missing values are median-imputed inside the saved pipeline (never zero-filled).
    • 

  • Models: Logistic Regression (baseline), Decision Tree, Random Forest. Best by ROC-AUC is saved with its preprocessing.
    • 

  • Nutrition engine is rule-based. Nutrient values show "Nutritional data unavailable" until filled from a verified source (e.g. IFCT 2017). Costs are illustrative.
    • 

  • Escalation thresholds are examples and need clinician approval. No diagnosis, no medication advice.
